## 10/10 Product Direction

- Primary motivation metric:
  - top-level Motivation should show perfect-day streak, best perfect-day streak, perfect days, and consistency;
  - per-habit Details should show each habit's own days hit, current streak, best streak, and consistency;
  - for quit habits, show both clear-day consistency and current streak after slip recovery.
- Status model:
  - distinguish `done`, `partial`, `missed`, `rest`, `slip`, `manual time`, `timer time`, `backfilled`, and `archived`;
  - do not overload `skipped` to mean every non-done state.
- Timed habits:
  - one visible source of truth for today's total;
  - manual time is an absolute external source, not an additive command;
  - only one active timer by default unless a future brief explicitly chooses multi-timer behavior.
- History:
  - start with details/timeline and compact status chips before a heavy dashboard;
  - later add calendar/heatmap, period summaries, notes, and explicit non-score trend metrics.
- Reset stats:
  - reset motivation metrics per habit through server-canonical reset events, not by deleting check-ins;
  - show a clear `Since <date>` boundary and keep pre-reset history explainable;
  - support multiple reset boundaries with dates, current post-reset metrics by default, and a Calendar Comparison path for complete history instead of hiding or deleting historical check-ins.
- Data confidence:
  - sparse Motivation data should still show concrete numbers;
  - use a small amber `Early data` explanation for fewer than 7 measured days that could count toward Perfect Days, not vague `Not enough history` copy.
- Micro Sessions linkage:
  - Micro Sessions remain separately tracked until an explicit future slice offers opt-in `Make recurring habit`, `Track as habit`, or `Link to habit`;
  - Micro Session is the one-off or saved short routine/mini-program and may contain one or more source Dryland Sessions/blocks, while Habit is the recurring commitment/rule;
  - Habit linkage should point to stable Micro Session routine identity, not title text, a single source Dryland Session title, or transient current-plan rebuild state;
  - source Dryland Session edits and `Update current micro session` must preserve completed/skipped history and must not break the Habit linkage or reinterpret old Habit credit;
  - no automatic Perfect Day linkage, silent habit creation, automatic historical backfill, duplicate overcounting, or hidden midnight check-in writes.
- Habit lifecycle:
  - keep existing edit/archive behavior stable in polish slices;
  - broader delete/restore/edit semantics need their own data-boundary brief.
- UI:
  - habit cards should be scan-first: title left, metadata grouped away from title, today's progress line under title, and one clear primary action;
  - mobile spacing must respect fixed nav and admin notes.
- Sound:
  - opt-in local preference only;
  - one short sound for target reached and mark done;
