- A reviewed absence date must not reappear in later review prompts across day/browser/device unless the product explicitly scopes review acknowledgement as local-only and accepts that limitation.
- A weekly or monthly any-day habit should count on the day it is performed; if the period ends unmet, the miss belongs to the period-end day unless a future product decision changes that model.
- Future habit check-ins must be rejected by protected write paths, not only hidden by UI.
- Future habit definition start dates must be rejected against server-derived effective local `today`, even when a client sends a matching future `selectedDate`.
- Habits, Today/Home, and Calendar must agree on the same `today`, selected date, and week boundary contract.
- Changing device/request timezone must not rewrite existing `check_in_date`, `review_date`, reset boundaries, or stable IDs.
- User-facing progress on action surfaces should be count-first; percentages belong to analysis/comparison surfaces unless a child explicitly justifies otherwise.
- Calendar count labels must make their denominator and object explicit: due habits, active habits, Perfect Day habits, micro units, swim sessions, or a deliberately named aggregate.
- Calendar week totals must not say only `No sessions` when the row is also visually summarizing non-session signals unless the label clearly means swim sessions only.
- Local-only storage is acceptable for transient UI and non-sensitive preferences, but not for user-visible historical truth that must survive browser/device changes.
- Unknown definition modes/types/statuses must not be coerced into known success-bearing values or accepted by direct definition/check-in/reset mutations or new linked credit; exact provenance-safe retraction of an existing Micro-derived credit remains allowed on undo. Child AA closed this H-080 gap in PR `#1251`.
- Raw unsupported definition values may remain in the private user export and a bounded support diagnostic, but must not be emitted to analytics or normal logs.
- Child AC closes category/operator/unit/cadence/schedule/target fallback: explicit unknown values and every partial-null cadence, duplicate, or malformed shape are unsupported; valid target shapes retain their intentional null fields, and only the exact all-null cadence tuple with known unique weekdays gets compatibility.
- Workflow acknowledgement and day classification are different truths: `status = reviewed` may close a prompt, while only an explicit supported `day_status = not_tracked` may exclude that whole local date from evidence-bearing metrics.
- `not_tracked` creates no `habit_check_ins`, is neither Done, Missed, Rest day, Slip, Perfect Day nor a clear Quit day, contributes no timed/count/source totals, and never changes `lastTrackedDate`.
- Performance over a range uses only known evaluation units; coverage separately reports `included known units / potential units`. With zero included units, the result is `No tracked data`, never `0%` or `100%`.
- A `not_tracked` date is a neutral hard streak boundary: it earns and protects no streak, is not labelled a failure, and a streak cannot bridge unknown evidence. Any later supported check-in wins and clears the marker; clearing that check-in must not silently resurrect the old marker.
- Weekly/monthly any-day periods remain success when recorded check-ins already meet the target; otherwise unknown dates make the period unknown only when they could change the result. If the target is mathematically unreachable even with all unknown dates, the period may remain missed.
- Child AB may create markers only for server-derived review candidates currently visible in the selected ISO week. H-071 owns any discovery or queue expansion into older weeks/all history; bounded consumers must still honor an already stored marker when their selected range includes it.
- Every changed Habits child must prove the affected object level: habit definition, check-in, period summary, review acknowledgement, reset boundary, or derived view model.

## 10/10 Product Direction

- Primary motivation metric:
  - top-level Motivation should show perfect-day streak, best perfect-day streak, perfect days, and consistency;
  - per-habit Details should show each habit's own days hit, current streak, best streak, and consistency;
  - for quit habits, show both clear-day consistency and current streak after slip recovery.
- Status model:
  - distinguish `done`, `partial`, `missed`, `rest`, `slip`, `not_tracked`, `manual time`, `timer time`, `backfilled`, and `archived`;
  - do not overload `skipped` to mean every non-done state.
  - keep review workflow acknowledgement separate from a whole-day evidence classification; do not store `not_tracked` as a synthetic per-habit check-in.
- Timed habits:
  - one visible source of truth for today's total;
  - manual time is an absolute external source, not an additive command;
  - only one active timer by default unless a future brief explicitly chooses multi-timer behavior.
- History:
  - start with details/timeline and compact status chips before a heavy dashboard;
  - later add calendar/heatmap, period summaries, notes, and explicit non-score trend metrics.
- Reset stats:
  - reset motivation metrics per habit through server-canonical reset events, not by deleting check-ins;
  - show a clear `Since <date>` boundary and keep pre-reset history explainable;
  - support multiple reset boundaries with dates, current post-reset metrics by default, and a Calendar Comparison path for complete history instead of hiding or deleting historical check-ins.
- Data confidence:
  - sparse Motivation data should still show concrete numbers;
  - use a small amber `Early data` explanation for fewer than 7 measured days that could count toward Perfect Days, not vague `Not enough history` copy.
- Micro Sessions linkage:
  - Micro Sessions remain one-off/separately tracked by default, while PR `#1017` provides explicit opt-in `Make recurring habit` linkage;
  - Micro Session is the one-off or saved short routine/mini-program and may contain one or more source Dryland Sessions/blocks, while Habit is the recurring commitment/rule;
  - Habit linkage should point to stable Micro Session routine identity, not title text, a single source Dryland Session title, or transient current-plan rebuild state;
  - source Dryland Session edits and `Update current micro session` must preserve completed/skipped history and must not break the Habit linkage or reinterpret old Habit credit;
  - no automatic Perfect Day linkage, silent habit creation, automatic historical backfill, duplicate overcounting, or hidden midnight check-in writes.
- Habit lifecycle:
  - keep the shipped non-destructive edit/end/history/restore behavior from PR `#1013` stable in later slices;
  - hard delete/permanent deletion still needs a separate data-boundary decision.
- UI:
  - habit cards should be scan-first: title left, metadata grouped away from title, today's progress line under title, and one clear primary action;
  - mobile spacing must respect fixed nav and admin notes.
- Sound:
  - opt-in local preference only;
  - one short sound for target reached and mark done;
  - no sound for slip, reset, archive, undo, or destructive flows.
