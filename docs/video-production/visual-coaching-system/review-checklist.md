# Review Checklist

## Purpose

Every review export must prove that the video teaches clearly, looks like FreeSwimming, and works in the intended format before it is approved.

## Pre-Review Requirements

- Production ID exists.
- Format suffix is in the filename.
- Export state and revision are in the filename.
- Source footage path is not public.
- No private customer/member data is visible or audible unless explicitly approved.
- Captions are present if the output requires them.

## Visual Review

Check:

- swimmer remains the visual priority,
- overlay does not cover head, hands, catch, body line, or wall contact,
- logo is visible but subtle,
- text is readable on phone,
- arrows and lines are thick enough after compression,
- mistake/fix meaning is not color-only,
- no unnecessary decorative effects,
- underwater footage has enough contrast.

## Format Review

### `16:9`

- Course lesson feels complete.
- Split-screen or comparison labels are clear.
- Captions and lower thirds do not collide.
- Desktop/tablet playback is readable.

### `9:16`

- Phone playback is readable.
- Platform UI zones are considered.
- Only one main teaching point is active.
- Crop preserves the key movement.

### `1:1`

- Swimmer remains centered enough.
- Labels are short.
- Top and bottom areas are not crowded.
- Before/after or mistake/fix comparison is understandable quickly.

## Audio Review

Check:

- voiceover is intelligible,
- pool sound does not compete,
- music does not compete,
- no private or unwanted audio is included,
- loudness is reasonable for web/social review.

## Caption Review

Check:

- captions are readable on phone,
- captions do not collide with coaching overlays,
- line breaks are natural,
- technique terms are consistent,
- captions remain localizable later.

## Educational Review

Check:

- viewer knows what to look at,
- the objective is clear,
- normal speed appears before heavy analysis where practical,
- slow motion and freeze frames clarify rather than interrupt,
- drill connects to the correction,
- final practice cue is actionable.

## Fail Conditions

Do not approve if:

- key movement is covered,
- social crop hides the teaching point,
- text is too small on phone,
- logo placement distracts,
- captions and coaching labels collide,
- mistake/correction meaning is color-only,
- export naming is ambiguous,
- FCP recipe claims are unverified,
- private footage/source paths are exposed.

## Approval Record

For each reviewed export, record:

```text
production_id
format
filename
revision
review_date
reviewer
approved
required_changes
notes
```
