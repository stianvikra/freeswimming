# Brand Extension

## Principle

FreeSwimming video production extends the existing FreeSwimming brand. It does not replace it.

The video layer should feel:

- premium,
- calm,
- technical without being clinical,
- swimmer-first,
- readable in water,
- consistent across course and social formats.

## Video Roles

| Brand element              | Video role                                            | Notes                                                     |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `symbol-*`                 | watermark, compact corner mark, wall-placement source | Use when the video already names FreeSwimming elsewhere.  |
| `lockup-domain-*`          | intro/outro, course title card, final frame           | Use when recognition matters.                             |
| `lockup-tagline-*`         | method statement, closing frame, course bumper        | Use sparingly.                                            |
| `tagline-inline-*`         | short branded transition                              | Only when it does not compete with coaching copy.         |
| `full-lockup-horizontal-*` | wide export intro/outro or sponsor-style frame        | Best for 16:9.                                            |
| `full-lockup-vertical-*`   | tall social intro/outro when space allows             | Best for 9:16 if swimmer footage is not active behind it. |

## Logo Placement

### Watermark

- Default: top right for `16:9`, top right or upper safe area for `9:16`, top right for `1:1`.
- Keep it small enough to never compete with the swimmer.
- Use white/reverse variants over dark water and primary/dark variants over light backgrounds.
- Never place a watermark over:
  - the swimmer's head,
  - hand entry,
  - catch path,
  - body line,
  - wall push-off,
  - timing cue area.

### Intro And Outro

- Use a clean lockup on a solid or footage-backed frame with controlled contrast.
- Keep intro short enough for lesson pace:
  - course lesson: `1.5-3.0s`,
  - social cutdown: `0.5-1.5s` or skip if the first frame already brands clearly.
- Outro should prioritize the viewer's next action or practice cue, not a decorative brand flourish.

### Lower Third

- Use lower thirds only for human names, drill names, or technique focus.
- Avoid lower thirds when captions already occupy the bottom safe zone.
- Keep logo treatment minimal: symbol only or no logo if the watermark is already visible.

### Wall Logo Treatment

Wall-placement logo effects are allowed later only as a deliberate production asset or verified FCP recipe. They must:

- look physically plausible,
- match perspective,
- preserve pool realism,
- avoid covering swimmer motion,
- be optional, not a default overlay.

## Color Rules

- Use blue for emphasis and branded cues, not as a full-screen tint.
- Use white for overlays on underwater footage when contrast is needed.
- Use ink/slate for light title cards, documents, and non-footage frames.
- Mistake/correction states must not rely on color alone:
  - pair color with label, icon, shape, or motion difference.

## Typography Rules

- Use Manrope.
- Keep text short and instructional.
- Avoid dense paragraphs over footage.
- Prefer:
  - `Focus: Early catch`
  - `Keep hips high`
  - `Press water back`
- Avoid:
  - long explanations,
  - decorative typography,
  - negative letter spacing,
  - tiny annotation text.

## Do Not Use

- New logos or sub-brands.
- Generic stock-video design language.
- Heavy gradient cards over the swimmer.
- Decorative blur or glow that makes water look muddy.
- Logo over key movement.
- Brand effects that make the viewer look away from technique.
